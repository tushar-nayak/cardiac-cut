WHAT THE ADDITIONS DO, IN ONE LINE EACH

  connectivity  a patch must be one connected piece of surface. Disconnected
                patches went to zero across 30 aortas; they had been carrying
                up to 85% of a case's delta K.
  fan gate      a patch whose normals span past 90 degrees has wrapped and no
                Monge fit describes it, though R2 and eta both pass on one.
  orphan        surface-aware re-homing of vertices orphaned by a split.
                Tested, no measurable benefit, DEFAULTS OFF.
  seed          the k-means seed is a parameter, so a case can be partitioned
                several independent ways and averaged. One partition is a draw:
                re-seeding moves delta K by ~16%; 8 partitions gives +/-6%.

TWO THINGS TO KNOW

  These change delta K for every branched geometry. Anything already published
  from this code will move.

  Averaging does not make mesh densities comparable. delta K drifts ~21% across
  a 4x change in face count because min_patch_size is a vertex count. Compare
  only at a fixed target.
